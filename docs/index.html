DPhil Candidate · University of Oxford

I'm Omar Ahmed, a DPhil student currently in my 2nd year at the University of Oxford. I focus on music recommendation systems, specifically how the concept of discovery can be better understood through user study, the creation of design criteria and an evaluation framework based on this understanding, and the development of a system that abide by these criteria.
